Darshan Options

  1. General Darshan: Free with potentially long wait times.

  2. Sugam Darshan: A paid service with shorter wait times, requiring advance booking on the official portal.

  3. VIP Darshan: The quickest darshan option, also requiring advance booking and a higher fee.

Seva Options

  1. Online Donations: Devotees can make financial contributions through the Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Teerth Kshetra (SRJBTK) portal, with options to view and download donation receipts.

  2. Aarti: The Aarti service can be experienced and possibly booked online through the SRJBTK's NextGen Online Services.
